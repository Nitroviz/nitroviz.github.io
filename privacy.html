Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

1. Overview

NitroViz YouTube Uploader is a tool designed to help users prepare and manage YouTube publishing workflows. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when the application is used.

2. Information We Access

When YouTube account functionality is enabled, the application may request access to information and actions necessary to perform the features selected by the user, such as managing YouTube videos and related publishing metadata.

The application does not request access to information that is unrelated to its YouTube publishing functionality.

3. How Information Is Used

Information accessed through Google and YouTube services is used only to provide the functionality requested by the user, including video publishing and management features.

We do not sell personal information or use Google user data for advertising purposes.

4. Data Storage

The application is designed to minimize unnecessary storage of user information. OAuth credentials and access tokens should be handled through the application's secure authentication mechanism and are not intentionally exposed publicly.

5. Third-Party Services

The application may interact with Google APIs and YouTube services. Their use is subject to Google's applicable policies and terms.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. Information is shared with third parties only when necessary to provide a requested service, comply with applicable law, or protect the security of the application and its users.

7. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by the application. However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

8. User Control

Users can stop using the application and revoke its Google/YouTube access through their Google account settings.
